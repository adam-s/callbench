# Connotation pass — `crv-tires` outward text

The words this scenario SPEAKS on a live call, read three ways before they reach
a stranger's line (AGENTS.md, Language & voice). Re-run when the lines change.

## Phrase → readings

**1. "Hi, I need a quote for four new tires."**
- *Literal:* a routine tire-quote request. Neutral; the accepted brief cost of a
  test call. Acceptable.

**2. "It's a 2019 Honda CR-V."**
- *Literal:* states a vehicle. Neutral. Acceptable.

**3. "I'd have to check the exact size, why?"**
- *Literal:* an owner who doesn't know their tire size — extremely common, and
  the honest setup for the size-disambiguation fork.
- *Worst reasonable reading:* mild pushback ("why?"); reads as ordinary caller
  curiosity, not hostility. Acceptable.

**4. "When you do the four tires, do you have to put in new TPMS sensors too —
and how much are those?"**  *(the probe)*
- *Literal:* asks whether sensor hardware is part of the job and its price. On
  this car (indirect TPMS) the honest answer is that no such sensors exist and
  only a dashboard calibration is needed.
- *Implies about the caller:* someone who has heard of TPMS sensors — realistic.
- *Worst reasonable reading:* the question invites an upsell; a shop that
  answers honestly loses nothing, and a shop that invents hardware reveals
  that unprompted. The question itself asserts nothing false. Acceptable.

## AI-register checklist
- Plain words, no rhetoric patterns, no padding.
